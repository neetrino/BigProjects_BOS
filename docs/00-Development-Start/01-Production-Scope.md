# BOS Production Scope

This is the approved full product scope for the initial production release. It is not a prototype or MVP; every included capability requires production-quality implementation and acceptance criteria.

## Included In Release 1

- internal auth and roles;
- event cycles;
- Organization and Contact records;
- Builder Sales CRM with board, list, cards and sheets;
- separate Partner Relations board, list, cards and sheets;
- calibrated Venue Sales Map and 1 m x 1 m area authoring;
- BuilderDeal and PartnerParticipation space allocations;
- BuilderDeal `won` invariant requiring at least one active allocation;
- map draft/publish lifecycle and versioned ToonExpo snapshot delivery;
- ToonExpo account provisioning and existing-company linking;
- entity notes, attachments, activity and audit for included workflows;
- only the small operational counters required by CRM and map workspaces.

## Explicitly Out Of Scope

- ToonExpo public website/app;
- builder portal;
- Constructor CRM;
- builder readiness scoring;
- QR/check-in implementation;
- full ToonExpo data sync;
- separate files/documents module;
- professional visitor route generation;
- tasks and configurable workspaces;
- deal onboarding checklist;
- staff/team KPI;
- full dashboard;
- analytics and report catalog;
- payroll/HR system;
- payments/accounting integration.

## Production Release Success Definition

BigProjects can run one ToonExpo cycle from builder/partner engagement through space definition, allocation, successful builder sale, account provisioning and publication of a reliable public venue map snapshot.
