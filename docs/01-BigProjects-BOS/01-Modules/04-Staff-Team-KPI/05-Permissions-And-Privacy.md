# Permissions And Privacy

## Role Access

Recommended:

- BOS Admin: full team KPI;
- BOS Staff: own work and allowed team views;
- BOS Viewer: read-only summary if permitted.

## Privacy

KPI is internal management data.

Do not expose staff KPI to ToonExpo builders, partners or public users.

## Editing

Most KPI data is calculated from source modules.

Admin can edit staff metadata:

- team;
- role;
- active/inactive;
- manager/owner if used.

Operational metrics should not be manually edited unless stored as explicit KPI notes/adjustments later.

## Audit

Audit:

- role changes;
- staff active/inactive changes;
- team assignment changes;
- manual KPI adjustments if introduced later.
