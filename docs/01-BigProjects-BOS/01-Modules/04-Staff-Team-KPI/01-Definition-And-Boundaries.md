# Definition And Boundaries

## Definition

Staff / Team KPI tracks internal team responsibility, workload and performance indicators.

It answers:

```text
Who is responsible for what, what is completed, what is blocked, and where does management need attention?
```

## In Scope

- staff list;
- staff profile/work summary;
- assigned deals;
- assigned tasks;
- onboarding item responsibility;
- completed/open/blocked task counts;
- approved participant counts by manager;
- provisioning responsibility if assigned;
- simple KPI summary/status;
- team-level KPI overview.

## Out Of Scope In v1

- payroll calculation;
- bonus calculation;
- HR employee records;
- attendance tracking;
- time tracking;
- complex performance review workflows;
- internal social feed.

## Source Modules

KPI reads from:

- Deals;
- Tasks;
- Deal Onboarding Checklist;
- ToonExpo Provisioning;
- Event Cycles;
- Reports.

KPI should not duplicate source data.

## KPI Principle

KPI must be explainable.

If a score exists, users should be able to see which real records contributed to it.
