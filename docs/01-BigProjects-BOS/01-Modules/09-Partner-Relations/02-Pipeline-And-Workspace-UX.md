# Pipeline And Workspace UX

## Main Screens

```text
Partner Board
Partner List
Partner Participation Sheet
Organization Sheet
```

The Partner Board reuses the common BOS Kanban component, drag/drop behavior, filters and sheet shell. It does not reuse BuilderDeal field definitions or transition rules.

## Card

The compact card shows:

- organization name;
- partner category;
- current stage;
- responsible staff member;
- venue space indicator when assigned;
- ToonExpo provisioning indicator when applicable.

## Sheet

The sheet contains:

- organization and contacts;
- cycle and partner category;
- stage and responsible user;
- participation conditions;
- optional area allocations;
- notes, attachments and activity;
- provisioning state.

## Filters

- event cycle;
- stage;
- partner category;
- responsible staff;
- has venue space;
- provisioning status.
