# Acceptance Criteria

- Partner Relations has a separate board and list from Builder Sales.
- Partner cards open a partner-specific sheet.
- Partner stages and fields are not BuilderDeal stages and fields.
- Builder Sales counts and reports exclude PartnerParticipation records.
- Partner space is optional.
- Partner allocations support organization, custom label and hidden public display modes.
- A confirmed partner can trigger idempotent ToonExpo provisioning.
- Notes, attachments and activity remain available without creating a general task module.
