## Purpose

The Dashboard is the main overview screen for BigProjects managers and staff.

## In Scope

- active deals;
- active participants;
- open tasks;
- blocked processes;
- staff/team KPI summary;
- participant onboarding progress;
- ToonExpo account provisioning status;
- high-level reports.

## Out Of Scope

- editing constructor CRM sales data;
- managing public ToonExpo pages;
- separate document/file archive.

## Main Users

- BigProjects owner/management;
- BigProjects managers;
- internal team leads.
