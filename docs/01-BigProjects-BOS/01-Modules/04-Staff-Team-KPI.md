## Purpose

Staff / Team KPI tracks BigProjects team performance.

## In Scope

- personal KPI;
- team KPI;
- assigned companies/participants;
- assigned tasks;
- completed tasks;
- in-progress tasks;
- blocked tasks;
- readiness responsibility;
- manager performance score.

## Out Of Scope

- payroll/bonus calculation unless explicitly added later;
- employee HR system;
- separate internal social feed.

## Main Views

- team KPI list;
- manager personal KPI page;
- assigned participant/project list;
- completed/in-progress/blocked task counts.
