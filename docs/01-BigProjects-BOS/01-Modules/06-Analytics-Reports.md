## Purpose

Analytics / Reports gives BigProjects visibility into internal performance and ToonExpo preparation.

## In Scope

- deal reports;
- participant reports;
- task/process reports;
- staff KPI reports;
- onboarding progress;
- blocked work;
- ToonExpo account provisioning status;
- exports if needed.

## Out Of Scope

- deep product analytics for public users;
- detailed constructor CRM editing;
- accounting/reporting unless added later.
